# Agile Ceremonies
  - **Sprint Planning**
    A meeting held at the **start of each sprint** where the team decides:
    - What work will be done in the sprint (sprint goal and backlog items).
    - How it will be accomplished (high-level task breakdown, approach).
  - **Agile Meeting (Daily/Weekly)**
    - **Daily:**
      - Also called **Daily Stand-Up** or **Daily Scrum**.
      - Short meeting (max 15 mins) **every day** of the sprint.
      - Each member answers:
        1. What did I do yesterday?
        2. What will I do today?
        3. Are there any blockers?
    - **Weekly:**
      - Some teams hold additional **weekly alignment or refinement meetings**, especially in hybrid agile setups.
      - Used for deeper backlog grooming, technical sync, or stakeholder alignment. 
  - **Sprint Review**
    A meeting held at the **end of the sprint** to:
    - **Demonstrate completed work** to stakeholders.
    - Gather **feedback**.
    - Decide **next steps or changes** for upcoming work.
  - **Sprint Retrospective**
    A meeting held after the **Sprint Review and before the next Sprint Planning**, where the team reflects on:
    - **What went well?**
    - **What didn’t go well?**
    - **What improvements can be made in the next sprint?**
